We are thrilled to welcome all families—both new and returning—to another wonderful year of Waldorf-inspired education at Pacífico Internacional!

## What to Expect This Year

This school year brings many exciting opportunities for growth, learning, and community connection. Our dedicated team of educators has been preparing thoughtfully to create nurturing spaces where every child can thrive.

### Key Dates to Remember

- **First Day of School**: August 18, 2025
- **Back-to-School Night**: August 25, 2025
- **Fall Festival**: October 15, 2025

## Our Commitment

At Pacífico Internacional, we remain committed to:

- Honoring each child's unique developmental journey
- Fostering creativity and imagination through hands-on learning
- Building a strong, supportive community of families and educators
- Connecting children with nature and the rhythms of the natural world

We look forward to walking alongside you and your children on this beautiful educational journey.
