We are thrilled to welcome all families—both returning and new—to the 2025-2026 school year at Pacífico Internacional!

## A Year of Growth and Discovery

This year promises to be filled with meaningful learning experiences, creative exploration, and the nurturing community spirit that defines our school. Our dedicated faculty has been preparing thoughtfully to guide each child on their unique educational journey.

## What to Expect

- **Enriched Curriculum**: Building on our Waldorf-inspired approach with new artistic and practical activities
- **Community Events**: Regular festivals, parent workshops, and family gatherings
- **Nature Connection**: Expanded outdoor learning opportunities in our beautiful Tamarindo setting

## Important Dates

Please mark your calendars for our upcoming orientation sessions and the first day of classes. Check our school calendar for all important dates.

We look forward to a wonderful year together!
