We are delighted to announce that our Morning Garden program is now accepting enrollments for the upcoming semester!

## About Morning Garden

The Morning Garden is our gentle introduction to school life for our youngest learners. In this nurturing environment, children ages 2-4 experience:

- **Rhythmic Daily Activities**: Songs, movement, and creative play that support healthy development
- **Nature Immersion**: Daily outdoor exploration in our tropical garden setting
- **Artistic Expression**: Watercolor painting, beeswax modeling, and other sensory-rich activities
- **Social Development**: Learning to play and share in a loving community

## Program Details

- **Ages**: 2-4 years old
- **Schedule**: Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM - 12:00 PM
- **Class Size**: Small groups with dedicated caregivers

## How to Enroll

Contact our admissions office to schedule a visit and learn more about the Morning Garden program. We welcome families to experience our warm community firsthand.

Spaces are limited, so we encourage interested families to reach out soon!
