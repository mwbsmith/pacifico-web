Our beloved Morning Garden program is now accepting enrollments for the upcoming semester!

## About Morning Garden

Morning Garden provides a gentle, nurturing introduction to school life for our youngest learners. In this warm and supportive environment, children ages 2-4 experience:

- **Circle time** with songs, movement, and fingerplays
- **Free play** with natural materials and open-ended toys
- **Outdoor exploration** in our beautiful garden spaces
- **Seasonal crafts** and artistic activities
- **Healthy snack time** with wholesome, organic foods

## Program Details

- **Ages**: 2-4 years old
- **Schedule**: Monday-Friday, 8:30 AM - 12:00 PM
- **Location**: Our dedicated early childhood building

## Why Choose Morning Garden?

Our program is rooted in Waldorf early childhood principles, which emphasize:

1. Learning through imitation and play
2. Establishing healthy rhythms and routines
3. Nurturing the senses through natural materials
4. Building social skills in a mixed-age setting

## How to Enroll

Spaces are limited to ensure each child receives individual attention. Contact our admissions team to schedule a visit and learn more about joining our Morning Garden community.
