We are excited to share that our faculty team continues to grow with talented, dedicated educators who share our passion for Waldorf-inspired education.

## Our Teaching Philosophy

At Pacífico Internacional, our teachers are more than instructors—they are guides, mentors, and companions on each child's educational journey. Our faculty members bring:

- Deep training in Waldorf pedagogy
- A love for children and their development
- Creative approaches to teaching and learning
- Commitment to ongoing professional development

## New Additions to Our Team

This year, we welcome several new educators who bring diverse talents and experiences to our community. Each has completed extensive Waldorf training and shares our vision of nurturing the whole child.

## What Makes Our Faculty Special

Our teachers understand that:

1. **Every child is unique** - We honor individual learning styles and developmental timelines
2. **Learning should be joyful** - We bring wonder and beauty into our classrooms
3. **Community matters** - We build strong relationships with families
4. **Nature is our teacher** - We integrate outdoor learning into daily life
