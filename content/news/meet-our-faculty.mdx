Pacífico Internacional is proud to introduce our expanding team of dedicated educators for the 2025-2026 school year.

## Our Teaching Philosophy

Every member of our faculty shares a deep commitment to Waldorf education principles and a passion for nurturing the whole child—head, heart, and hands. Our teachers bring diverse backgrounds and experiences, united by their dedication to creating meaningful learning experiences.

## New Faculty Members

We are excited to welcome several new teachers who bring fresh perspectives and expertise to our community:

- Experienced Waldorf-trained educators from around the world
- Specialists in music, movement, and the arts
- Bilingual teachers supporting our English-Spanish immersion program

## Ongoing Professional Development

Our faculty participates in continuous professional development, including:

- Waldorf teacher training programs
- Collaborative curriculum planning
- Artistic skill workshops
- Child development studies

## Meet the Team

We invite all families to visit our Team page to learn more about each of our wonderful educators. You can also meet them in person during our upcoming open house events.

We are grateful to have such a dedicated team guiding our students!
